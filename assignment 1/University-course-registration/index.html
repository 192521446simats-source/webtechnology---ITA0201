Registration Instructions

  1. Enter your valid register number.
  2. Provide your full name.
  3. Enter your university email address.
  4. Select your department.
  5. Select your current semester.
  6. Choose one or more available courses.
  7. Verify the registration summary.
  8. Click Complete Registration.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Student must be enrolled in an active semester.
  • Required course prerequisites must be satisfied.
  • Student must follow the maximum credit limit.
  • Registration must be completed before the deadline.
